# Student tracking and team formation

TaldeBot supports a student's continuing record and the specific projects in which they participate. Teachers see shared tracking information through their current teaching responsibility.

## Language

**Student**:
One person whose identity continues across projects, enrollments, names and email changes.
_Avoid_: Project student, roster identity

**Enrollment**:
A student's dated membership of a class in an academic period. Ending an enrollment preserves its earlier membership.
_Avoid_: Class member as a permanent person

**Project participant**:
A student's involvement in one project, including that project's attendance and survey state.
_Avoid_: Student when referring to project-local completion or absence

**Assessment**:
A dated self-assessment with its author, questionnaire version and collection context. It is a student's reported confidence, not an academic grade.
_Avoid_: Grade, objective ability score

**Preference submission**:
A student's dated, directed comfort, collaboration and avoidance choices for an academic period or a particular project. An explicit empty submission replaces earlier choices in that scope; an unasked section does not.
_Avoid_: Popularity, permanent avoidance label

**Pairing restriction**:
A teacher-managed mandatory separation rule with a scope, author and review date. It is distinct from a student's soft avoidance preference.
_Avoid_: Student avoidance as a mandatory rule

**Update request**:
An invitation to a named student to answer specified assessment or preference sections. Completion belongs to this invitation or its project.
_Avoid_: Global student completion

**Team draft**:
A proposed allocation that may be regenerated, compared or adjusted. It does not establish assignment history.
_Avoid_: Confirmed team, collaboration history

**Formation method**:
The teacher's chosen way to propose teams: survey-informed, random, manual or by term-time town. Mandatory pairing restrictions apply to every method; random formation ignores skills, optional preferences and previous teams.

**Term-time town**:
A student's teacher-confirmed town of residence during teaching, shared across simultaneous enrollments. A historical roster town is a suggestion until confirmed; absent or conflicting locations remain unknown for formation.

**Confirmed assignment**:
A teacher-adopted project allocation with dated membership periods and retained decision inputs. Revisions preserve earlier assignments.
_Avoid_: Verified collaboration, worked together

**Teaching responsibility**:
A teacher's current access to a degree or class, managed by a master administrator. It determines access to ongoing records and sensitive formation inputs.
_Avoid_: Global visibility, permanent historical access
