# Only confirmed assignments contribute to pairing history

Jon confirmed mandatory teacher restrictions, soft student avoidance and a teacher-selected balance between familiar and new collaborators. Drafts and legacy generated teams are excluded from confirmed history; confirmation retains decision inputs and dated revisions, and a pair is counted at most once per source project across those revisions. Mandatory rules are never relaxed silently, and an overdue review date flags a rule for review without cancelling it.
